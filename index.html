About KFNXT

Cleaner, smarter, more reliable energy

KFNXT is the energy brand of KF Integrated Energy Private Limited, part of KF Group. We help homes, businesses and institutions across India move to cleaner, smarter and more reliable energy, from first assessment to installation and after-sales support.

What we do

Six ways we power what's next

Solar Power

On-grid, off-grid and hybrid rooftop solar systems, from site survey and design to installation and net-metering support.

Battery Storage

Home and commercial battery energy storage (BESS) for backup power and better use of solar energy.

EV Solutions

EV charging points for homes, apartments, offices and commercial sites.

Smart Energy

Energy audits, monitoring and power electronics that cut waste and lower electricity bills.

Infrastructure

Electrical and energy infrastructure for new buildings and upgrades.

AI & Digital

Data-driven monitoring and reporting for energy systems.
