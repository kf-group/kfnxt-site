This policy explains how KF Integrated Energy Private Limited ("KFNXT", "we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you visit kfnxt.com or contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address and details of your energy requirement.
  • Messages you send us through our website, phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and browser type.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes and deliver our services.
  • To send service updates, appointment reminders and information you ask for, including on WhatsApp.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

WhatsApp communication

When you message us on WhatsApp, we use your WhatsApp number and messages to reply and to keep a record of your enquiry. You can ask us to stop messaging you at any time by replying STOP or contacting us below.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with KF Group companies, installation partners and service providers who help us deliver our services, and with authorities where the law requires it.

Storage and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we protect it with reasonable security measures.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to office@kfnxt.com.
